Sources were retrieved in full; setup pages were rendered and checked visually as well as textually. ## Review distinctions

- Eye to Eye names the person with the most unique eyes as the opening category reader. Players write answers simultaneously afterward. Its subjective criterion is excluded from the random mix.
- Math Medalist has two separate games on opposite sides of the board. Multiplication Zones uses a quick age-doubling challenge; Field of Hundreds uses a quick 100 + 90 + 10 sum. The mode-specific answer is excluded from the random mix.
- Trifusion assigns scorekeeping to the oldest player and the opening tile to the youngest. These roles were kept distinct.
- Sumology can use individual players or two teams. The same tile comparison identifies the starter; its second-lowest and subsequent comparisons are an official tie-break. SlideAscope and Prickly Path also provide explicit redraw/reroll tie-breaks.
- The other manuals in this batch do not state an opening tie-break; any random choice on the site is labeled a house suggestion. Endgame tie-breaks and later-round starting changes were not reused as opening tie-breaks.
- Nebulous Connections, Poles Apart and Team Digger remain discovery-only identities until a qualifying opening-player answer can be sourced. Their game-specific role or simultaneous/cooperative order is not converted into an invented starter.
- Phantom Seas also has a public SimplyFun rulebook, but it is outside these 54 accepted product identities and was not assigned a record in this batch.
