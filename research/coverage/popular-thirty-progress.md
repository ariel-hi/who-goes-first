# Popular game starting-rule batch — 2026-09-25

The requested 30 popular titles now have sourced English-edition records. Twelve were approved on `main` while this branch was in progress; this branch adds the other 18. Each new approved JSON record identifies its edition, source, exact PDF page, source SHA-256, clarification, and any unresolved gap. The corresponding draft remains in `research/games/` as a review trail.

The 18 new titles are outside the separate 1,333-game discovery inventory. They increase approved catalog rules from 800 to 818; inventory coverage remains 777 researched identities and 556 pending. No new rule enters the portable random mix: these openings depend on components, player choice, assigned roles, turn phases, team structure, or plain random selection.
