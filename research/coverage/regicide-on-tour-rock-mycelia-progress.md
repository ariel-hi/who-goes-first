Regicide came from its official publisher site. The On Tour, Rock Hard and Mycelia files were archived copies of publisher-produced rulebooks. Rock Hard’s PDF contains image-only pages, so its setup was read from the rendered page. The Mycelia PDF uses facing printed pages: PDF page 3 contains printed pages 4–5. Its mushroom cards, spore counters and Mother mushrooms identify the Split Stone Games strategy game BGG 378476; the Ravensburger deck builder is BGG 392023 and is not represented by this rule.
