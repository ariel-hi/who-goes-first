# Two original Library of Congress game boards — 2026-09-28

This source pass adds two physical board-game identities to the 5,253-name
directory baseline. They are scoped to the inspected historical issues; no
starting-player rule, BoardGameGeek ID, image or later-edition match is
approved. Local coverage rises to **5,255 identities**.

| Object | Decision and original evidence |
| --- | --- |
| [*Rambles Through Our Country: An Instructive Geographical Game for the Young*](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2006677460/) | Accept one 1890 American Publishing Company issue. The [original print](https://cdn.loc.gov/service/pnp/pga/03200/03272v.jpg) names the company as copyright claimant, prints the 1890 date and title, and shows a numbered U.S. map route. The Library identifies Schaefer & Weisenbach as lithographers, a different role. The Library's [map blog](https://blogs.loc.gov/maps/2019/10/may-the-best-player-win-geographical-board-games-of-the-past/) describes an accompanying instruction booklet, but no rule is transferred from it. |
| [*The Office Boy*](https://www.loc.gov/item/97196328/) | Accept one 1889 Parker Bros. Salem board. The [original board scan](https://tile.loc.gov/image-services/iiif/public:rbc:2017carson96328:0001/full/pct:25.0/0/default.jpg) prints the publisher, copyright date, title, rules panel, career route and Head of the Firm goal. The Library catalogs a physical 47 × 42 cm board and says its playing pieces and teetotum are missing. Later issues remain distinct leads. |

The accepted source URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in
[`publisher-identities.json`](publisher-identities.json) and rechecked by the
[batch validator](../../scripts/prepare-loc-two-board-games-batch.mjs).
Original scans were opened for inspection; no artwork or game-rule wording
was copied into the site. The Library lists no known publication restriction
for the *Rambles* original and no known U.S. restriction for the Rare Book
collection, while noting that use decisions remain the reuser's responsibility.
The two accepted names were absent from the active research and site source
projection before insertion.
