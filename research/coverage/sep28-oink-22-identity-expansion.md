# Twenty-two Oink Games analog identities — 2026-09-28

This bounded primary-publisher batch adds 22 exact physical tabletop-game products to the discovery directory. The reviewed baseline at `077eed9a` had 5,222 identities; the staged registry has 5,244. No starting-player rules or BoardGameGeek IDs are inferred. The directory remains a growing sample, not a complete worldwide inventory.

The [official Oink Games English game lineup](https://oinkgames.com/en/games/) provided 85 analog-game product links. Eight had exact normalized-title matches in the existing directory. Thirty-one other products were individually reviewed from their original publisher pages: 22 accepted and nine held. Each accepted page returned HTTP 200 at the exact lineup URL; its HTML title and two focal headings agree on the name, its Game Description identifies physical play components, and its Credit section states a publisher release year. The catalog and product response hashes and byte counts are bound to every accepted record in `publisher-identities.json`. Original responses and receipts are retained in ignored `artifacts/oink-analog-sep28/`; only identity facts enter the build.

The accepted records claim only the named Oink analog product and its page's publisher release year. They do not assert an original worldwide publication year, another printing or territory, an English rulebook, a BGG mapping, or any rule transfer. All accepted names and product URLs passed merged-registry collision checks; held products are absent from the public directory.
