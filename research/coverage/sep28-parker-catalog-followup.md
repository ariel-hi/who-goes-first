# Parker Brothers catalog follow-up — 2026-09-28

Five more physical products from the original [Parker Brothers *Illustrated
Catalogue of Standard Games*](https://gamesandpuzzles.org/images/trade-catalogs/parker-brothers/1889_Parker_Brothers_catalog_-Strong_Parker_Brothers_Inc._1889_95033143-from_TheStrong_%28handmarked_1892_incorrectly%29_-prep_2025-01-02_6MB.pdf)
were accepted as identity-only records. Its printed pages lack a year; the
archive indexes the scan as 1889. No exact manufacture dates are asserted.

| Product | Physical distinction and decision |
| --- | --- |
| *The Garrison Game* | Accept the siege board on printed page 12 (PDF page 8). The illustrated publisher label and description identify a large boxed folding board with turned maple pieces; 50 attacking pieces oppose three defenders. |
| *The Railroad Game*, 50-cent issue | Accept the folding **wooden** railway landscape board on printed page 13 (PDF page 8), with teetotum and playing pieces in the box. |
| *The Railroad Game*, Popular Edition | Accept the separately headed 25-cent issue on printed page 13 (PDF page 8). Its color-and-gold lithographed folding board folds into a box with pieces and directions. Same title alone does not prove exact rules or component correspondence with the wooden-board issue. |
| *The Grocery Store Game* | Accept the 50-cent large-box card edition on printed page 14 (PDF page 9). Its illustrated publisher label and premium grocery cards distinguish its package; the publisher says it plays in the same manner as *Corner Grocery*. |
| *Corner Grocery* | Accept the separately advertised 25-cent card-and-money issue on printed page 15 (PDF page 9), illustrated by its labeled box and described as about 40 priced grocery cards plus about 100 play-money pieces. |

The [preceding five catalog products](sep28-parker-catalog-identities.md) stay
separate; all ten cite the same original publisher volume. Every shared-source
collision has an explicit, revision-bound distinct-product review. Exact and
normalized names were absent from the active discovery and publisher-identity
files before insertion. Other catalog products, and any later prints or
modern games with similar names, remain unlinked. No starting-player rule,
BoardGameGeek ID, publisher art or game-rule text is transferred to the site.
