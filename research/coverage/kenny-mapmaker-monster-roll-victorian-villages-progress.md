# Kenny G, Mapmaker, Monster Crunch, Roll for Adventure, Victorian Masterminds and Villages of Valeria

Checked on 2026-09-24. Six publisher-authored English manuals supplied six more opening instructions. Four are preserved by the [Dice Tower Library archive](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dtompkin/archive/dtlib/dtlibrary.html); [Thames & Kosmos](https://www.thamesandkosmos.com/manuals/full/692988_Roll_for_Adventure_Manual.pdf) and [CMON](https://www.cmon.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Rulebook_VictorianMasterminds.pdf) host the other two. The cited pages were rendered and checked, and the individual records contain source hashes.

| Game | Opening instruction | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kenny G: Keepin' It Saxy Game | Most recently played a musical instrument | The saxophone marker holder starts the Sax phase; the marker passes left each round. |
| Mapmaker: The Gerrymandering Game | Most recently voted | The first three players place one, two, then three borders on their opening turns. |
| Monster Crunch! The Breakfast Battle Game | Most recently ate a real bowl of cereal | Later round starters are determined by who remains in the previous round; the next hand starts with the player who ran out of cards. |
| Roll for Adventure | Player determined most heroic | Cooperative game with a turn order; the manual offers no judging test. |
| Victorian Masterminds | Best evil laugh | The manual offers no judging test. |
| Villages of Valeria | Any method; main setup suggests most recently built something | Its quick reference says to pick at random, so both printed suggestions are retained. |

All six are included in the portable random mix. The main Villages of Valeria setup explicitly leaves the choice open; the quick reference's random wording does not erase that flexibility.
