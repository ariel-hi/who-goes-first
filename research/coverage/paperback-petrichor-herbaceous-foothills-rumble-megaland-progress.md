# Paperback, Petrichor, Herbaceous, Foothills, Rumble Nation and Megaland

Checked on 2026-09-24. Six publisher-authored English rulebooks preserved in the [Dice Tower Library archive](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dtompkin/archive/dtlib/dtlibrary.html) supplied six more opening instructions. The relevant pages were rendered and visually checked; source hashes are recorded in the individual research and approved records. The archive's BGG links were matched by numeric ID to the discovery inventory. The archive hosts the publisher manuals; it did not author their rules.

| Game and edition | Opening instruction | Distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paperback, original Fowers Games edition | Most recently finished reading a novel | This is not Paperback Adventures or the later anniversary edition. |
| Petrichor, Mighty Boards printing | Most recently caught in the rain, or any other selection method | Clouds are placed in reverse play order after the starter is selected. The publisher's original download URL currently fails, but its indexed text matches the archived booklet. |
| Herbaceous, Pencil First Games base game | Most recently cooked with or planted herbs | The four-player team variant instead gives the first turn to the team containing the youngest player. |
| Foothills, Lookout Games 2019 | Choose a starter; the most recent person to dig soil in their garden is the printed example | The starter keeps the chit for the whole game; initial resources are chosen in reverse order. The publisher's indexed English manual agrees with the archived booklet. |
| Rumble Nation, 2019 English original edition | Most recently visited a castle, or choose randomly | This booklet has three dice and 24 tactics cards, distinguishing it from a later Deluxe printing. |
| Megaland, Red Raven Games 2018 | Most recently played a video game | The token passes left each Night. |

All six have a portable opening criterion and are included in the random mix. Where the manual gives an alternative or an example, the site preserves that flexibility. None of these manuals supplies a tie procedure for the respective recency criteria.
