# Thirty-one SimplyFun product identities — 2026-09-28

This bounded publisher-catalog batch adds 31 standalone physical board, card and tile game products to the discovery directory. The reviewed baseline at `5ad62c7d` contained 5,191 identities; the merged registry contains 5,222. Of those, 1,103 have a researched edition and 4,119 remain without one. These are discovered identities, not a complete worldwide game inventory or new starting-player rules.

The [SimplyFun sitemap](https://simplyfun.com/pages/sitemap) supplied product leads. Each accepted title has a separate HTTP 200 response from its canonical [publisher product page](https://simplyfun.com/collections/all-games). The page H1 and Product JSON-LD agree on the title, SimplyFun brand, SKU and GTIN. Its Game Includes section identifies physical components and a rules booklet. The exact response SHA-256 and byte count are in `publisher-identities.json`; the raw responses, sitemap response and retrieval receipts are retained only in ignored `artifacts/simplyfun-next-batch-sep28/`. No product description or image was copied into the public build.

Research-only receipts and responses stay out of the public build.
