# Bob Ross games, The Brigade, Firenze, Inuit, My Happy Farm, Trellis and Veggie Garden

Checked on 2026-09-24. Eight publisher-authored English rulebooks supplied eight more catalog answers. Seven complete booklets or sheets are preserved by the [Dice Tower Library archive](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dtompkin/archive/dtlib/dtlibrary.html); Quined Games hosts its [Firenze second-edition manual](https://www.quined.nl/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Firenze-Rules-EN-WEB.pdf), which is byte-identical to the archive copy. Relevant pages were rendered and checked; each record contains its source hash.

| --- | --- | --- |

| The Brigade | Most recently set something on fire | That player resolves three starter Events before their first Action phase. |
| Firenze | Most recently went up a tower | Quined Games’ 2018 second edition gives the starter two white bricks and later players progressively more. |
| Inuit: The Snow Folk | Enjoys cold weather most, or any other method | This is the base-game setup, separate from optional modules. |
| My Happy Farm | Most recently fed their pet | Portal Games English edition, distinct from the 2011 original publisher credit. |
| Trellis | Last watered a plant, or won the previous game | Turns pass left. |
| Veggie Garden | Last ate fresh vegetables | The Quick Simple Fun game by Kelly North Adams. |
